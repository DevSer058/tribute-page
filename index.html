Carlos Slim Helú

Carlos Slim is a mexican businessman, engineer and philanthropist recognized worldwide as one of the richest men in the world.

Timeline of achievements and awards

  • 1940 – Born in Mexico City
  • 1966 – Owned several real estate and commercial businesses by age 26
  • 1990 – Led the acquisition of Telmex, becoming a telecom leader
  • 1999 – Founded América Móvil
  • 2007–2013 – Ranked the richest person in the world by Forbes
  • 2014 – Became major shareholder of The New York Times
  • 2019 – Carlos Slim Foundation reached over $4 billion invested in social projects
